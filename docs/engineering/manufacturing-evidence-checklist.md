# M0 manufacturer evidence checklist

This is an evidence request list, **not an assertion that suppliers have quoted**.

## Bare SoC / semiconductor vendor
- Exact orderable P/N, authorized distributor, MOQ, lifecycle, lead time and sample terms.
- Public vs NDA-restricted reference schematics, pinouts, DDR app notes, boot ROM and PMIC sequencing.
- Redistribution rights for boot firmware and required binaries.
- Errata, thermal characteristics, package drawings and PCN/EOL policy.

## PCB fabricator
- Controlled impedance stack-ups and tolerance; minimum trace/space/via; via-in-pad/HDI availability.
- DDR and PCIe length/skew constraints support, fabrication test, coupons, panelization and yield expectations.
- Price breaks at prototype/pilot/production quantities, tooling charges, manufacturing country.

## Assembly house
- BGA pitch and placement capability, X-ray/AOI coverage, rework, moisture-sensitive handling, stencil and fixture costs.
- Component procurement policy, traceability, DFM review, pilot yield data, warranty and acceptance criteria.

## Compliance / fulfillment
- Determine applicable EU/EEA legislation and conformity obligations for finished configurations and accessories.
- EMC precompliance lab access, RoHS material declarations, WEEE producer responsibilities, packaging and electrical safety evaluation.
- Product liability, insurance, VAT, consumer returns, support and spare-unit reserves.

## Evidence log fields
Owner, request date, contact/company, document/quote link, revision, validity date, currency, quantities, limitations, confidentiality and reviewer. Never publish confidential quotes without permission.
