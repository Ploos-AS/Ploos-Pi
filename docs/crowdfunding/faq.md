# Public FAQ — early project stage

**Can I buy Ploos Pi now?** No. This is concept/feasibility work.

**Are these Raspberry Pi compute-module carriers?** No. All three are planned as original bare-SoC SBC designs.

**Which CPU do you use?** No SoC selected; selection follows public evidence and procurement review.

**What will it cost?** No verified retail pricing. A cost model is being developed.

**Will Linux run?** ARM64 Linux is a design requirement, not yet a tested claim on custom boards.

**Is a crowdfunding campaign planned?** It is being evaluated, contingent on validated prototypes, manufacturing and fulfillment evidence.

**Are the hardware files open?** Open-hardware release is intended, subject to third-party IP rights and license review. Publication schedule and exceptions must be explicit.

**When will it ship?** There is no shipping commitment or delivery estimate.

**How will supporters hear about risks?** Published risk register, test evidence and material changes, including failures and schedule slips.
