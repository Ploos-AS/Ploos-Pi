# Crowdfunding readiness and public trust

**No campaign launched, no preorders accepted, no promised delivery date.** This document defines future readiness, not a commitment to raise funds.

## Campaign gates (all required)
- [ ] At least one working, independently documented engineering prototype of the product offered.
- [ ] Public unedited boot/video demonstrations, measured benchmarks, temperature and power data.
- [ ] Exact revision-controlled schematics, BOM and source release plan; clear proprietary dependency disclosures.
- [ ] Written quotes for manufacturing, testing, packaging and fulfillment at campaign volumes.
- [ ] Verified component availability and credible replacement/contingency plans.
- [ ] Test plan with published pass/fail evidence and unresolved defects.
- [ ] Unit economics at conservative/baseline/optimistic pledge scenarios, including taxes, fees, scrap, warranty and returns.
- [ ] Compliance plan for Norway/EU/other target markets, including EMC, electrical safety, RoHS/WEEE and relevant CE obligations assessed for the *actual product configuration*.
- [ ] Independent DFM/DFT and manufacturing feasibility review.
- [ ] Reward scope, delivery estimates, refund policy, risks and update cadence reviewed.
- [ ] Separate go/no-go signoff by engineering, operations and finance.

## Ethical campaign language
Distinguish: **concept**, **simulated**, **prototype**, **validated**, **pilot**, **production**. Never present renders as photographed hardware. Annotate benchmark/price/power figures with method, date, board revision and uncertainty. Publish material setbacks promptly.

## Proposed updates
Monthly project progress before fundraising; during campaign and fulfillment, publish an agreed cadence with milestones, funds usage, risks and schedule changes. Avoid guaranteeing hardware specifications before measured prototypes exist.

## Gatekeeper
Do not collect pledges until prototype capability, manufacturability and total fulfillment costs are evidenced. Crowdfunding is not a substitute for engineering feasibility.
